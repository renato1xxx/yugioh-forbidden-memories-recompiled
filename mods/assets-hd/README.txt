Forbidden Memories HD (Unchiga, X@nder) for Yu-Gi-Oh! Forbidden Memories Recompiled v0.3.0

Extract this archive into the game folder (next to memories-pc or
memories-pc.exe) so that mods/assets-hd sits beside the other mods.
Turn the mod, or any of its parts, on or off in the Mods window.
Looks best at View > Internal 4x with HD text on.
For the anime-style card frame, turn on Anime card frame in the Mods window.
